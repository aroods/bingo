SMART Goals Bingo

Listen to the goal statement. Tap the SMART element it demonstrates or lacks.
Rules
  1. Every student receives one Bingo card.
  2. Lecturer reads goal statements aloud.
  3. Students mark boxes that match the SMART element demonstrated or missing.
  4. First to complete a full row, column, or diagonal shouts "SMART!".
  5. Lecturer continues to read until 5 people complete bingo.
  6. "Winners" discuss briefly why each statement fits or fails SMART criteria.
